# Cybersecurity Risk Assessment

## Project Context

NorthStar Professional Services is a fictional organization with approximately 75 employees. The organization handles client information and relies on information technology to support its daily operations.
As the organization has grown, its technology environment and security practices have developed over time. This project presents a cybersecurity risk assessment of that environment to identify and evaluate risks that could affect the organization.
The assessment is focused on risk identification and analysis. It is not a penetration test and does not involve active exploitation of systems.
## Assessment Scope

This assessment focuses on the systems, information, access controls, and third-party services that could materially affect NorthStar Professional Services if compromised, misconfigured, misused, or made unavailable.

The assessment includes:

- **Employee endpoints and access:** Employee laptops and the controls governing user identities, roles, privileges, authentication, authorization, and access throughout the employee lifecycle. Individual employees are not assessed separately.

- **Company files and storage:** The on-premises file server containing business records, including access controls, protection of information, and backup and recovery arrangements.

- **Cloud and customer systems:** Microsoft 365 and the cloud-based customer relationship management (CRM) system, with attention to access, configuration, authentication, and protection of sensitive information.

- **Network infrastructure:** The internal network, Wi-Fi, firewall, and associated controls protecting access to NorthStar's internal environment.

- **Privileged access:** Administrative privileges held by the two IT administrators and the controls used to protect, authorize, monitor, and attribute privileged activity.

- **Third-party access:** Vendors and service providers with authorized access to NorthStar systems or information, including the controls and responsibilities governing that access.

- **Public-facing website:** The externally managed company website and the risks its compromise, misuse, defacement, or unavailability could create for NorthStar and its customers.

Third-party operation of a service does not automatically remove that service from the assessment. Operational security responsibilities may be assigned to a service provider, but NorthStar retains accountability for understanding and managing the risks associated with using that service.
